Compliance & security

Every rule your desk answers to, written as it stands.

For Canada, CurrencyDesk maps FINTRAC's obligations onto the desk: every deal logged, and cash thresholds watched from the book. Canada is the only live country. Below is what the product does now, what is only in a pack, and where each rule comes from.

The desk

CurrencyDesk OS is the counter itself: you quote a rate, take the deal, and the ticket, the client and the cash position all move together. There is no separate compliance system to reconcile at the end of the day, because the deal record is the compliance record.

The rulebook

The country your desk is set up in decides which pack loads, and Canada is the only live pack. While the customer is at the counter, the desk checks the identification lines from the book and will not post a deal over a line until ID is on file. A large cash worksheet can be prepared. The desk does not send it.

KYC, not connected

Client verification is not connected yet. The desk does not scan a document, take a selfie, or screen a list. The prices below are listed for later. See KYC pricing.
